Cap Rate & Cash-on-Cash Calculator — Single-Family Home

Assumes 80% financed by default at the current 30-yr fixed rate (—%).  ·  Cap Rate = NOI ÷ Price  ·  Cash-on-Cash = (NOI − Annual Debt Service) ÷ Down Payment

Default prices & rents are approximate 2024–25 market estimates.   Cap Rate > 6% = generally good  ·  4–6% = marginal  ·  < 4% = low yield.   Cash-on-Cash > 8% = strong  ·  4–8% = fair  ·  < 4% / negative = cash-flow negative.
